Recent trend: 2022–2024

  • Wind/Earthquake: more site/location/exposure/soil/risk-category data and supplied coefficient tables.
  • ILD: both beams and frames, often with internal hinges; conceptual question appears too.
  • Approximate analysis: portal + cantilever dominate recent papers; factor method remains a backup.
  • Set 7: mill-bent portal application and space truss appear together/adjacent.
  • Set 8: continuous beam + cable remains one of the most stable repeated pairs.
  • Backlog: same eight families, but the printed two section blocks are reversed.
